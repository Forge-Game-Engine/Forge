Forge's demos, documentation and website use art, audio and fonts made by
the people below. Thank you to all of them.

## Sprites and textures

- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), all licensed
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
  - [UI Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack): the buttons, sliders, text
    inputs and panels in the UI demos.
  - [Fantasy UI Borders](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-ui-borders): the
    frames in the nine-slice, masks and sprite atlas demos.
  - [Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack): the particle
    textures in the particles demo.
  - [Pattern Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/pattern-pack): the backgrounds in
    the physics demos.
  - [Pirate Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-pack): the sprites in the
    pirate battle, texture atlases and sprite atlas demos.
  - [Puzzle Pack 2](https://kenney.nl/assets/puzzle-pack-2): the sprites in
    the brick breaker demo.
  - [Rolling Ball Assets](https://kenney.nl/assets/rolling-ball-assets): the
    balls and blocks in the physics demos.
  - [Simple Space](https://kenney.nl/assets/simple-space): ships, meteors and
    stars in the space demos.
- [Screaming Brain Studios](https://screamingbrainstudios.com/dl-seamless-space-backgrounds/):
  the nebula space background.
- [Wenrexa](https://opengameart.org/content/assets-free-laser-bullets-pack-2020):
  the laser bullets in the space shooter demo.
- [Squaremeapixel](https://squaremeapixel.itch.io/space-pack): the
  spaceships and asteroids in the space shooter demo.
- [CodeManu](https://codemanu.itch.io/vfx-free-pack): the explosion
  animation in the space shooter demo.
- [Overcrafted](https://overcrafted.itch.io/free-paid-vehicle-car-sprites-):
  the car body and wheels in the car demo.
- [Deep-Fold's Pixel Planet Generator](https://deep-fold.itch.io/pixel-planet-generator):
  the pixel-art planet in the rendering demos and blog.
- [OboroPixel](https://oboropixel.itch.io/characters-animations-asset-pack):
  the soldier sprite sheets in the sprite animation docs.
